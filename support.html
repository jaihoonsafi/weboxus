Frequently asked questions

What is Oxus?

Oxus is a weight-loss app focused on portion control: you log what is on your table (photo or text), and Oxus suggests how much of each food to eat in grams so you can stay in a calorie deficit without a restrictive meal plan.

How do I get the app?

Download Oxus from the App Store on your iPhone. A free trial is available when you subscribe.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions purchased through Apple are managed in Settings on your iPhone or iPad: Apple ID → Subscriptions → Oxus. We cannot cancel or refund purchases from here; Apple handles billing.

How do I delete my account or ask about my data?

You can delete your account from Profile settings inside the app. For privacy-specific questions, see our Privacy Policy or email us at the address above.
